SINCE 1947

A short history of Ferrari

1947

The first Ferrari

The 125 S, the first car to carry the Ferrari name, is built in Maranello, Italy. It has a 1.5L V12 engine.

1951

First Formula 1 win

José Froilán González wins the British Grand Prix at Silverstone, Ferrari's first victory in the F1 World Championship.

1969

Fiat joins

Fiat buys a major stake in Ferrari, giving the company the money to grow its road car production.

1988

The end of an era

Enzo Ferrari passes away at the age of 90. The F40 is the last Ferrari he oversaw.

2000

The Schumacher years

Michael Schumacher wins Ferrari's first drivers' title in 21 years, starting a run of five championships in a row.

2013

LaFerrari

Ferrari launches its first hybrid hypercar, combining a V12 engine with an electric motor.

2019

SF90 Stradale

The first plug-in hybrid Ferrari and the first series-production Ferrari with all-wheel drive.
